Sogang Spatial Economics & AI Lab

Research Group Member (Master's Student) · Lab Head: Hyunjoo Yang

The lab combines geospatial data, economics, and machine learning, building data from historical maps, archival records, and satellite images to study Korea's development.

I work on an agricultural prediction project using machine-learning methods and spatial data. I collected and constructed ground-truth datasets for maize and rice.

Nov 2025–Present

MIRACLE — Subnational Economic Data for South Korea, 1960–1989

Research Associate · Project Leads: BooKang Seol, Changkeun Lee, and Hyunjoo Yang

I contribute to a township-year economic panel for South Korea, 1960–1989, using municipal statistical yearbooks and archival records, with consistent geographic identifiers across administrative boundary changes. I completed the administrative-region data collection stage and coordinated a five-person data-collection team.

I am currently working on OCR-based extraction and processing of historical statistical records.
