Working Paper

Draft, Dec 2025 - Present

The Economic Impact of Submarine Cable Arrival on Local Economic Activity: Evidence from South Africa

Yoorim Son. Advisor: Hyunjoo Yang.

This project examines the economic impact of submarine cable arrival on local economic activity in South Africa. It uses district-level panel data, satellite-based proxies, and econometric methods including difference-in-differences and IV (2SLS).
